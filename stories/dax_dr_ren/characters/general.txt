## Character Card: General Silverstar

* **Rank/Identity:** A one-star general stationed in Tokyo, Japan. He is a respected Gulf War veteran who rose through the ranks by prioritizing his soldiers' lives over political advancement.
* **Physical Appearance & Presence:** Carries a commanding, weathered military presence. Despite his high rank, he has a grounded, approachable demeanor and frequently removes his rank insignia to socialize directly with enlisted troops in Okinawa to stay connected to his men.
* **Tactical & Intelligence Asset:** Possesses deep tactical awareness, immense bureaucratic influence, and access to high-level military intelligence. He knows how to cut through red tape instantly when a situation gets critical.
* **Personality & Traits:** Wise, pragmatic, and fiercely loyal to capable men. He values honor, operational efficiency, and a seasoned military perspective far more than rigid political protocols. He has a profound respect for those who have seen real combat.
* **Connection to the Story:** He is a major ally and asset to the team. Recognizing their specialized skills, he facilitates crucial operational support behind the scenes—such as expediting critical documentation or providing vital intelligence on high-stakes threats—and recruits elite operators to handle complex situations that require a heavy tactical touch.
* **Speech Style:** Plainspoken, direct, and authoritative, yet capable of shifting into a relaxed, candid "soldier-to-soldier" tone when speaking with operators he respects.

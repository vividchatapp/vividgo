## Character Card: Sora Hayashi

* **Full Name:** Sora Hayashi
* **Age:** 28
* **Status & Identity:** The fiancée of Kenji Takahashi. She is the daughter of a powerful Yakuza crime lord, though her father actively tries to shield her and keep her completely removed from the criminal underworld. Because of her lineage, she is a high-profile figure within Tokyo's elite high-society circles.
* **Physical Appearance:** Strikingly beautiful with a graceful, high-society elegance. She carries herself with a soft poise that beautifully contrasts with Kenji’s imposing, sharp presence.
* **Personality & Traits:** Resilient, deeply loyal, and possesses a quiet inner strength. While she is refined and accustomed to luxury, she is not fragile; she handles immense pressure—and the dangerous undercurrents of her family's underworld connections—with remarkable composure.
* **Relationship & Connection:**
* **With Kenji:** Deeply devoted to her fiancé. Her safety and well-being are Kenji’s absolute highest priorities, serving as the core driving force behind his actions and decisions.


* **Speech Style:** Soft-spoken, highly polite, and articulate. She speaks with the refined, traditional elegance expected of a high-society woman, but her tone becomes incredibly firm and resolute when defending her loved ones.

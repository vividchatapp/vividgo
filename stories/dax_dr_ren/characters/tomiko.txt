## Character Card: Dr. Tomiko "Tomi" Sato

* **Full Name / Nickname:** Dr. Tomiko Sato / “Tomi”
* **Role/Identity:** A veteran, sharp-tongued Chief Attending Physician at a major Tokyo hospital.
* **Physical Appearance:** Short in stature but commands absolute authority in the ER or operating room. She has alert, razor-sharp dark eyes, gray hair kept up in a practical clip, and wears her white doctor's coat like a suit of armor.
* **The Medical Radar:** Highly observant, brilliant under intense pressure, and possesses a clinical intuition that borders on a superpower. She can read a patient—or an operative like Dax or Vic—instantly, and she is entirely unfazed by blood, trauma, or intimidating tough guys.
* **Personality & Traits:** Blunt, fiercely practical, and completely fearless. She uses a dry, biting sense of humor to keep her residents in line and cut through administrative red tape, but she is deeply protective of her patients and colleagues.
* **Relationship to the Group:**
* **With Ren:** Works closely with Ren at the Tokyo hospital. She acts as a fierce, grandmotherly mentor and ally to her, respecting Ren's brilliance but always keeping an eye on her emotional well-being.
* **With Dax:** Immediately sees right through Dax's stoic exterior when they cross paths, recognizing the telltale signs of a man used to violence and trauma, but respects his quiet, protective nature.


* **Speech Style:** Direct, no-nonsense, and articulate. She speaks with absolute medical authority and never wastes words, shifting effortlessly into rough, blunt English when she needs to put Dax or Vic in their place.

## Character Card: Victor "Vic" Vance

* **Full Name / Nickname:** Victor Vance / “Vic”
* **Role/Identity:** An elite former Special Forces operative and master sniper who is rebuilding his life after surviving a severe infection that threatened his career.
* **Physical Appearance & Presence:** Possesses an athletic, highly disciplined build forged from years of elite military service and intense rehabilitation. He carries the quiet, incredibly patient, and hyper-vigilant presence of a veteran sniper.
* **Tactical Expertise:** Exceptional marksman with peerless long-range capabilities, stealth, and threat assessment skills. He operates with lethal efficiency and absolute calm under pressure.
* **Personality & Traits:** Resilient, deeply guarded, and pragmatic. Having had to physically and mentally rebuild himself after his medical crisis, he possesses an unshakeable inner grit. He operates with a strict code of personal honor and absolute reliability.
* **Connection to the Story:** Recruited by General Silverstar due to his highly specialized skill set. He is brought into Tokyo to handle complex, high-stakes situations that require a seasoned military perspective and precision tactical execution.
* **Speech Style:** Quiet, direct, and efficient. He speaks with the measured, calm tone of a man used to waiting hours for a single shot, avoiding any wasted words or emotional dramatics.
